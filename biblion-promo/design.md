## Overview

Biblion promo video with hyper-dynamic 25-second pacing. Light cream background with navy and gold accents. Aggressive pop/snap animations for maximum retention.

## Colors

Light cream (#F5F0E8) background conveys warmth and accessibility. Navy (#0D1B2A) for text and structure. Gold (#D4A853) for accents and highlights. White surfaces for cards.

## Typography

DM Serif Display for headlines — elegant, warm serif. DM Sans for body — clean humanist sans-serif. Weight contrast: 400 headline vs 400 body with bold labels.

## Elevation

White cards with subtle shadows on cream background. Gold accent lines and decorative elements. Minimal depth — flat premium aesthetic.

## Do's and Don'ts

- Do: Use light cream background for warmth
- Do: Keep animations aggressive and fast (0.3s max)
- Do: Use gold accents for visual hierarchy
- Don't: Use dark backgrounds except in phone mockups
- Don't: Use slow transitions — everything snaps
- Don't: Stack elements symmetrically
